First Group Departure

From Wisconsin to Thailand.

Temples, history, mountains, and islands — fourteen days across four destinations, organized for a Wisconsin travel circle and built to feel like a shared journey, not a tour group.

DatesOct 25 – Nov 7, 2027
Group Size8–14 Travelers
Investment$3,999 / Person
I

Bangkok

3 Nights

The Grand Palace and Wat Pho, a Chao Phraya river cruise, floating markets, and rooftop views.

II

Kanchanaburi

2 Nights

The Bridge on the River Kwai, the Death Railway, Erawan Falls, and a scenic train through the valley.

III

Chiang Mai

4 Nights

The old city's temples, a Thai cooking class, an ethical elephant sanctuary, and Doi Inthanon National Park.

IV

Koh Lanta

4 Nights

A four-islands tour, the Emerald Cave, sunset sailing, and rest on some of the quietest beaches in Thailand.

Included

  • English-speaking guides throughout
  • All hotel accommodations
  • Daily breakfast & three group dinners
  • Ground transportation and domestic flights in Thailand
  • Airport transfers & travellikeandy LLC concierge support

Not Included

  • International airfare
  • Travel insurance (strongly suggested)
  • Daily lunches & hotel incidentals
  • Personal expenses
  • Passport & visa fees, if applicable

A $500-per-person deposit is due September 1, 2026, and becomes non-refundable after October 1, 2026. Beyond that, refundability follows each hotel and experience's own policy — travel insurance is strongly suggested.
